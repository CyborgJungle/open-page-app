Archived pages should fit the screen

A saved webpage can carry the dimensions of the desktop where it was captured. The article should still fit comfortably on a smaller screen, with its heading, images and original typography intact.

Advertentie

Empty advertising spaces should disappear without removing the surrounding paragraphs. On a wider display, the page should sit in the centre rather than leave a large grey column along one side.

On a phone, images should scale down and the article should wrap naturally. Readers can scroll through the page while the Open Page toolbar stays available above it.
